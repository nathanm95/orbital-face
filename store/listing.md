# Orbital: appstore listing

## Name

Orbital

## Short description

The real solar system on your wrist. Shake it and the planets fall under real gravity, then glide home.

## Full description

Orbital puts the solar system on your wrist, and it is the real one. The Sun sits in the middle and all eight planets sit where they actually are today, worked out on the watch from its own clock. No internet and no lookup tables: the planets creep along their true orbits as the weeks go by, and the Moon rides beside Earth at tonight's phase.

Then shake your wrist. Every planet is knocked loose and falls under real inverse-square gravity. A gentle flick sets them drifting on wider ellipses; a hard one slingshots them round the Sun. A few seconds later they glide back to exactly where they belong.

- Real planet positions, recalculated every minute
- Orbits drawn as true ellipses, so Mercury and Mars visibly lean
- Shake to fling the planets, with motion trails
- The Sun's colour follows the time of day
- The Moon at its real phase
- Around 30 settings: 12 or 24 hour, clock at the top, middle or bottom, four date styles, battery as a percentage, an edge ring or only when low, a Bluetooth alert, step count, five colours, starfield, planet size, trails, the outer planets, and the physics itself (fling strength, gravity, and how long they take to settle)
- Easy on the battery: when nothing is moving it redraws once a minute
- Made for every Pebble, colour and black-and-white, round and rectangular, including Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2
